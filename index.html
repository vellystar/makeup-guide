Skin Profile

Combination skin is the duality of Hell itself — oily where you least want it, parched where you need glow. Master it, and your makeup lasts through any infernal climate.

Overall

Simultaneous oily + dry zones · Needs zone-specific care

What Is Combination Skin?

Combination skin features two opposing textures simultaneously — an oily T‑zone producing excess sebum by mid-day, and cheeks that lean dry-to-normal, sometimes feeling tight or flaky. Jakarta's humidity amplifies both extremes dramatically.

The key: zone-targeted application. Mattify the T‑zone while maintaining dewy hydration on the cheeks. Never treat the whole face identically.

💧

Hydration Strategy

Lightweight non-comedogenic formula all over. Richer cream on dry cheeks. Gel-based on T-zone. Hyaluronic acid is the hero — deep hydration without pore-clogging. Layer HA serum before moisturizer for maximum effect.

✦

Makeup Suitability

Serum skin tints and cushion foundations are ideal. Set T‑zone with powder only — never the cheeks. Clean & Clear Film before mid-day powder. Double-mist method (PIXY + L'Oréal) is your daily armour in Jakarta.

☀️

SPF — Non-Negotiable

Jakarta's UV index reaches 8–11 daily. UV stimulates sebum production — skipping SPF makes T-zone oilier. SPF 30+ every morning. Your SOMETHINC Ceraplump covers lip SPF beautifully.

⚗️

Key Ingredients

Niacinamide, Ceramides, Hyaluronic Acid, BHA (Salicylic Acid for T-zone), Centella Asiatica, Zinc, Green Tea. Avoid heavy comedogenic oils on T-zone.

Jakarta Climate

Tropical humidity pushes oily zones to peak shine faster. The Clean & Clear Oil Film + double-mist method is essential armour. Dewy cheeks actually look better in humidity than matte-everywhere.

Do's & Don'ts

✓ Blot before powder · Zone products · Double cleanse nightly · Drink water

✗ Over-powder cheeks · Skip moisturizer · Heavy comedogenic base · Hot water wash

◆ Know Your Canvas ◆

Makeup Finishes

Every product leaves a different finish on the skin. Knowing what each finish does — and which of your products delivers it — is the key to building a cohesive, intentional look.

Matte

Zero Shine · Flat · Velvet

Absorbs light completely, leaving no visible shine or reflection. Creates a flat, velvety skin surface. Controls oil and minimizes the appearance of pores. Ideal for oily T-zone areas. Can look dry on combination skin if applied all over — best used strategically.

Your Matte Products

TIMEPHORIA Lumina Cushion Second Date Loose Powder PIXY Matte Mist

Best used on T-zone only for combination skin — apply TIMEPHORIA on oily zones, leave cheeks untouched for a natural contrast.

Satin / Natural

Balanced · Skin-Like · Soft Lustre

The middle ground between matte and dewy. Reflects a soft, diffused light — not shiny, not flat. Skin looks natural and healthy. The most universally flattering finish for combination skin because it looks realistic and controlled on both the oily and dry zones simultaneously.

Your Satin Products

TIRTIR Mask Fit Red Cushion SKINTIFIC Cover Glow Cushion

Both cushions land in a natural satin zone — flawless coverage that photographs well and holds up in Jakarta's humidity.

Dewy / Glowing

Luminous · Radiant · Fresh

Reflects light visibly for a lit-from-within, hydrated appearance. Skin looks plump, glassy, and fresh. Essential for dry cheeks on combination skin. Can read as "oily" on T-zone so it must be controlled with powder or matte products on those zones only.

Your Dewy Products

SKINTIFIC Cover Glow Cushion ESQA Serum Skin Tint NPure Glow Up Oil

Apply these on the cheeks and outer face. Always set the T-zone with powder after to prevent the glow from reading as unintentional shine.

Blurred / Soft-Focus

Pore-Less · Airbrushed · Diffused

A soft-focus effect that visually erases pores and fine texture, like a photo filter applied directly to skin. Neither shiny nor flat — it simply appears smooth and refined. Achieved through silicone-based or blurring polymer formulas that scatter light in all directions.

Your Blurred Products

ESQA Minimalist Serum Skin Tint Second Date Translucent Powder

ESQA creates a soft-focus blurred skin base. Second Date pressed over it locks the blurred effect in place and stops T-zone oil from breaking through.

Glass / Glossy

High-Shine · Lacquered · Wet-Look

Maximum light reflection — skin or lips look lacquered, wet, or glassy. Achieved with oils, serums, and glossy formulas. On the face it creates a "glass skin" effect. On lips it amplifies colour and makes them look fuller. This finish requires confident product layering — too much reads as greasy rather than glassy.

Your Glossy Products

NPure Glow Up Oil Glad2Glow Lip Serum L'Oréal Setting Mist (glass effect)

A final sweep of L'Oréal setting mist on a minimal base creates the "glass skin" effect. NPure oil on cheekbones and Glad2Glow on lips adds the glossy contrast points.

Stained / Tinted

Sheer · Buildable · Transfer-Resistant

A wash of pigment that mimics the appearance of natural flushing or naturally tinted skin. Unlike opaque colour, a stained finish lets the skin texture show through slightly, creating an "effortlessly flushed" look. Most effective on well-conditioned skin — uneven texture or dryness can cause patchiness.

Your Stained Products

Time Phoria Stellar Dust Lip Stain SOMETHINC Ceraplump (tinted) Nivea Rich Care & Color

Cherrion stain is your most potent stain — it genuinely dyes the lip surface. Apply to well-conditioned lips (after overnight Ceraplump treatment) for the most even result.

◆ Finish Layering for Combination Skin

The ideal combination skin look uses at least two finishes simultaneously — one matte on the T-zone to control shine, and one satin or dewy on the cheeks to avoid that parched, flat look. The art is in the contrast: TIMEPHORIA Matte cushion on the nose and forehead, ESQA Skin Tint or SKINTIFIC Glow on the cheeks, a blurring pass of Second Date powder on T-zone only, then a final glass-skin sweep of L'Oréal mist to marry everything together. The result is effortlessly real — not one homogenous texture, but the texture your skin would have on its best day.

◆ The Dissolution Ritual ◆

Makeup Removal

Every product in your collection was designed to last. Removing it deserves the same intention — done wrong, it's the fastest way to clogged pores, damaged skin, and ruined lashes.

◆ The Cardinal Rule of Removal

Never — under any circumstances — sleep in your makeup. TIRTIR Mask Fit, all three waterproof brow products, and the setting mists are specifically formulated not to budge. They require deliberate, correct removal. One skipped night clogs pores, causes breakouts, and deposits pigment into fine lines. The grimoire demands the full ritual every time.

⚠ Requires Oil-Based Remover

TIRTIR Mask Fit Cushion

Mask-Fit adhesive technology bonds to skin — micellar water alone won't dissolve it. Must use oil cleanser or balm cleanser with 15+ sec contact time.

L'Oréal Infallible Setting Mist

24-hr waterproof hold means it resists water-based removers. Oil breaks the bond effectively without over-rubbing the skin.

Azarine + Luxcrime Brow Products

Both are 12+ hr waterproof formulas. Cotton pad soaked in oil cleanser, press on brows for 10 sec, then gently wipe — never scrub.

Time Phoria Stellar Dust Lip Stain

Lip staining formula — the pigment penetrates the top lip layer. A cotton pad soaked in oil or micellar water held over lips for 20 sec loosens the stain without drying rubbing.

✦ Easier to Remove

SKINTIFIC & TIMEPHORIA Cushions

Standard cushion formulas lift easily with micellar water or a gentle cleansing balm. No extended contact time needed.

ESQA Serum Skin Tint & ONLYOU BB

Light-coverage formulas — micellar water on a cotton pad removes these cleanly in one pass.

NPure Glow Up Oil & Lip Products

Oil-based lip products (NPure, Glad2Glow, Nivea, Ceraplump) wipe away easily with a damp cloth or single micellar pad.

JADED Eyeshadow

Butter Cheese formula blends out easily but the shimmers require a gentle eye-specific remover — oil-based is best to avoid irritation.

1

Lip Removal — Dissolve the Stain

Micellar Water or Oil Cleanser · Cotton Pad

Start with the lips — they're easy to forget and the Cherrion lip stain needs time to dissolve. Saturate a cotton pad with micellar water or oil cleanser and press firmly over the lips for 15–20 seconds without rubbing. Let the product do the work. Once the stain has loosened, gently wipe in one direction. Repeat if needed. Finish with a dab of NPure Marigold Peach oil or Ceraplump Valentine to re-condition lips immediately after removal.

◆ The longer the stain has set (e.g. end of a long day), the more soak time it needs. 20–30 seconds of still contact is more effective than vigorous scrubbing and far kinder to the lip skin.
2

Eye & Brow Removal — The Delicate Zones

Oil Cleanser or Dedicated Eye Makeup Remover · Separate Pads Per Eye

Soak two separate cotton pads in oil cleanser or bi-phase eye makeup remover. Lay one flat over each closed eye and hold for 10–15 seconds — the oil dissolves the waterproof brow formulas and the butter cheese eyeshadow pigments simultaneously. Then, using gentle downward strokes only, wipe once. Use a fresh pad if any residue remains. For the brow area specifically: one soaked pad pressed for 10 seconds over each brow, then a single upward wipe following hair direction. Never rub brows back and forth — this causes premature hair loss.

◆ Use separate cotton pads for each eye — cross-contamination is a real risk for eye infections, especially with oily T-zone skin that harbours more bacteria.
3

Face — First Pass with Oil Cleanser

Oil Cleanser or Cleansing Balm · Dry Hands, Dry Face

Apply oil cleanser or cleansing balm directly onto dry skin with dry hands — water prevents the oil from bonding to makeup properly. Massage in slow circular motions for a full 60 seconds, paying extra attention to: the nose (where product accumulates in pores), the T-zone (where TIRTIR Mask Fit bonded most firmly), and the hairline and jawline (where cushion edges accumulate). The setting mist, cushion formulas, and SPF all emulsify and lift during this step. Add a small amount of water to emulsify the cleanser — it will turn milky — then rinse thoroughly.

◆ The "dry hands, dry face" rule is not optional for oil cleansers. Water blocks the oil from making contact with makeup — always apply to completely dry skin for full effectiveness.
4

Second Cleanse — Purify the Pores

Gentle Foam or Gel Cleanser · Lukewarm Water

Even after a thorough oil cleanse, residue remains — SPF, setting mist polymer film, and sebum from the day's wear. Follow immediately with a gentle foaming or gel cleanser. 60 seconds of soft circular massage focusing on the T-zone and nose. For combination skin, the second cleanse is essential: the oily T-zone accumulates more layers of product throughout the day than the dry cheeks, and these layers must all be removed to prevent blackheads. Rinse with lukewarm — never hot — water.

5

Check — The Cotton Pad Test

Clean Cotton Pad · Micellar Water

After double cleansing, swipe a clean cotton pad soaked in micellar water gently across the face. Check the pad. If it comes away clean: the removal is complete. If colour, pigment, or brown residue appears on the pad: repeat Steps 3 and 4. This test is particularly important when wearing TIRTIR Mask Fit (which can leave adhesive film), the L'Oréal setting mist, or when you've used the baking technique with heavy powder. A clean pad = a clean canvas for tomorrow.

◆ The cotton pad test protects your skin long-term. Residual makeup left overnight — even trace amounts — accumulates in pores, causes hyperpigmentation, and accelerates dullness in combination skin.
6

Micellar Eye Rinse

Eye-Safe Micellar Water · Optional but Recommended

After the main cleanse, use eye-safe micellar water on a fresh cotton pad to gently wipe the lash line and inner corners — these areas trap the most residual mascara and eyeshadow fallout, even after oil cleansing. The JADED shimmer shades in particular leave glitter particles that migrate to the inner corner and lower lash line. A single gentle swipe along the lash line and inner corner removes these remnants completely.

7

Tone — Restore pH Balance

Alcohol-Free Hydrating Toner · Pat In

Double cleansing temporarily disrupts the skin's acid mantle — apply a hydrating, alcohol-free toner immediately after to restore pH balance and prep the skin for any overnight treatments. Pat gently into the face using the fingertips, focusing extra application on the dry cheeks. The toner also acts as a "rinse check" — if it stings even slightly, some cleanser remains on the skin and a final rinse is needed.

8

Overnight Lip Seal — Ceraplump Valentine

SOMETHINC Ceraplump Tinted Lip Balm — Overnight Treatment

Apply a generous layer of Ceraplump Valentine on completely bare, clean lips as the final step. At night, without any lip stain or gloss underneath, the ceramide formula makes direct contact with the lip skin and works undiluted. This overnight seal repairs micro-tears from the day's wear, rebuilds the lip's moisture barrier, and ensures tomorrow's lip products — especially Cherrion stain — absorb more evenly and last significantly longer. It also gradually brightens any lip pigmentation with consistent use.

◆ Healthy, ceramide-conditioned lips are the silent reason your Cherrion stain applies beautifully and your Pumpkin Frost gloss sits evenly. The overnight ritual earns its results by morning.

◆ Removal Do's

Always double cleanse — one cleanse never fully removes a full day of waterproof makeup, SPF, and setting mists

Press, then slide — hold the remover on the area first to dissolve, then wipe gently in one direction

Use separate pads per eye — cross-contamination can cause eye irritation and infections

Do the cotton pad check — always verify a clean canvas before moving to skincare

Seal lips after removal — bare clean lips absorb the overnight ceramide treatment most effectively

✗ Removal Don'ts

Never rub or scrub — especially around the eye and brow area where the skin is thin and delicate

Never skip removal — even one night of sleeping in TIRTIR Mask Fit causes visible pore congestion

Never use wipes as the only step — makeup wipes move product around the face and leave significant residue in pores

Never use hot water — strips the skin's protective barrier and overstimulates T-zone oil production

Never rush the lip stain step — aggressive rubbing at the Cherrion stain thins and chaps the lip skin over time
