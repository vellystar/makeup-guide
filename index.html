✦ Skin Profile
Understanding your combination skin — the foundation of every routine.
🌗
Combination Skin

Combination skin features both oily and dry zones. The T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) tends to be oilier with larger pores, while the cheeks may feel dry, normal, or balanced. It's the most common skin type globally and requires a balanced, adaptable routine.

🔬
What Causes It?

Genetics primarily dictate combination skin. Sebaceous glands are more concentrated and active in the T-zone, producing excess oil, while the cheeks have fewer glands, leading to dryness. Hormones, weather, and skincare habits also influence sebum production throughout the day.

⚡
Key Challenges

Balancing hydration without increasing oiliness. Matte products can over-dry cheeks; rich products can worsen T-zone shine. Finding a foundation that adapts to both zones, controls shine, and doesn't cling to dry patches is essential — and tricky.

💧
Hydration Needs

Combination skin still needs hydration everywhere — even oily zones. Skipping moisturizer can trigger compensatory oil overproduction. Use lightweight, gel-based or serum-type hydration, and consider zone-specific application for richer products on dry cheeks.

☀️
Sun Protection

SPF is non-negotiable for combination skin. Look for lightweight, non-comedogenic formulas with matte or satin finishes to prevent midday greasing. Reapply every 2 hours, especially on oily zones where sunscreen may break down faster due to sebum.

🌸
Makeup Tips for Combo Skin

Primer is your best friend — use pore-minimizing primer on T-zone and hydrating primer on cheeks. Choose buildable, medium-coverage foundations. Set the T-zone with loose powder; avoid heavy powder on cheeks. Always set with a matte setting spray on oily zones.

✦ Combination Skin Makeup Pro Tips
Prep skin with a hydrating toner before makeup. Use blotting paper — not more powder — to control T-zone oil midday. Always double-cleanse at the end of the day. Hyaluronic acid serum applied to damp skin helps balance both oily and dry zones without adding grease. For makeup longevity, always finish with a setting spray on your whole face.

✦ Understanding Undertones

Undertone is the subtle hue beneath your skin's surface that stays constant regardless of tan, season, or breakouts. Identifying your undertone helps you choose foundation shades that blend seamlessly instead of looking grey, orange, or washed out. In this diary, every shade is labelled C Cool, N Neutral, or W Warm.

🔵 Cool

Pink, rosy, or bluish veins on wrist. Silver jewellery suits you better than gold. Burns easily in the sun. Look for shades labelled C — porcelain, pink-beige, rosy nudes, berry, and plum tones will complement you.

⚪ Neutral

Bluish-green veins — a mix of both. Both gold and silver flatter you. You can pull off most shades labelled N. Vanilla, ivory, and beige tones with neither obvious warmth nor coolness suit you perfectly.

🟠 Warm

Greenish or olive-toned veins. Gold jewellery looks most natural. Tans easily. Look for shades labelled W — peach, caramel, honey, coral, and golden-beige will give you the most seamless, glowing match.

✦ Your Shades: Your owned foundation shades — Vanilla (SKINTIFIC & ESQA), 17C Porcelain (TIRTIR), Bare & Creme (TIMEPHORIA) — suggest a cool-to-neutral undertone. The 17C code confirms a cool undertone preference for that base layer, while Vanilla and Creme sit in the neutral-warm zone. This means your skin sits beautifully at the cool-neutral crossover, and both C and N shades will work for you depending on the look.

✦ Morning Routine Overview — Natural Look
Perfect for everyday wear: dewy, fresh, and effortlessly polished. Focuses on skin prep, skin-tint base, light brows, and a subtle glazed lip. Total time: approximately 15–20 minutes.
1
Skincare First — Moisturize & Sunscreen
5 min

Always begin with a clean, moisturized face. Apply your regular moisturizer and allow it to absorb for 2–3 minutes. Follow immediately with sunscreen (at least SPF 30). This creates the ideal canvas for makeup adhesion and ensures all-day skin health.

💡 Pro tip: Use a lightweight, gel moisturizer on your T-zone and a slightly richer formula on the cheeks. Allow skincare to fully sink in before any makeup — rushing this step leads to pilling.
2
SOMETHINC Ceraplump Tinted Lip Balm SPF 25
30 sec

Apply the lip balm first so it has time to sink in while you do your base. The ceramides need a few minutes to condition the lips. This SPF-infused lip balm protects and primes your lips so any subsequent lip product looks smoother and more even.

💡 Apply generously and blot once with a tissue after 2–3 minutes. This removes any slippery excess while leaving conditioned, matte-primed lips perfect for colour.
3
ESQA Minimalist Blurring Serum Skin Tint (Vanilla)
2 min

Apply 2–3 drops of the skin tint onto clean fingertips or a damp sponge. Press and pat into skin starting from center outward — forehead, nose, cheeks, and chin. ✦ Morning Routine — Full Glam Look
For special occasions, events, or when you want to feel extra royal. Full base, sculpted brows, eyeshadow, and a bold lip. Total time: approximately 30–45 minutes.
1
Skincare Prep — Full Routine
5–8 min

Complete your full skincare routine: cleanser, toner, serum, moisturizer, and sunscreen. Allow each layer to absorb. For glam days, consider adding a pore-minimizing primer on the T-zone after sunscreen — this creates a smooth canvas for full-coverage cushion foundations.

💡 Silicone-based primer fills pores and creates a smooth surface. Apply only where needed (T-zone, around nose) rather than all over to avoid a slippery base on normal/dry cheek zones.
2
SOMETHINC Ceraplump Lip Balm + NIVEA Smoky Rose
1 min

Apply Ceraplump first as your SPF lip base. Then layer the Nivea Smoky Rose on top — this creates a tinted, pre-stained lip that makes the final bold lip colour last significantly longer. The Smoky Rose also acts as a sophisticated base for deeper lip shades.

ESQA Skin Tint (Vanilla) — Base Layer
2 min

Apply ESQA as a first base layer. This serum-tint creates an even, blurred canvas and infuses the skin with serum actives before the heavier cushion goes on top. This double-base technique allows more natural-looking full coverage — the ESQA prevents the cushion from sitting on top of dry patches.

TIRTIR Mask Fit Red Cushion (17C Porcelain)
3 min

💡 If going for a natural-glam look, use SKINTIFIC Cover Glow Cushion instead for a more radiant finish. Reserve TIRTIR for events where maximum wear time is needed.

ONLYOU Concealer Brightening BB Cream

Conceal under eyes, any stubborn blemishes that show through foundation, and around the nostrils if needed. Second Date Loose Powder (Translucent) — Baking + Setting
1 min + 3 min wait

For glam, use the powder more generously: bake under eyes (apply, wait 3–5 min, dust off), set T-zone with a press-and-roll motion, and lightly dust cheeks. While the powder bakes, proceed with brows and eyes — the powder works while you do other steps.

💡 The baking technique sets concealer, reduces creasing, and gives under-eyes a bright, photogenic finish that lasts all day. Always use a fluffy brush to dust off baking powder to avoid disturbing the makeup beneath.
7
Brows — Luxcrime (Dark Brown) + Azarine (Eyeppuccino) + Hanasui (Brown)
5 min

💡 Define the brow tail sharp and crisp. The arch should be clearly sculpted. For a face-lifting effect, extend the tail slightly upward at a 45° angle. Set brows with clear brow gel if available.

Add shimmer to the center of the lid by patting with fingertip. Apply glitter to the inner corner and center for a blinding finish. Line the waterline with the darkest shade for depth.

💡 Start light and build dark — you can always add depth but can't easily remove. Use small, circular blending motions for the crease to create a seamless gradient. Tap (don't sweep) the shimmer shade for maximum payoff.
9

3 min

💡 A clean base (from NIVEA Smoky Rose applied earlier) makes the stain look more vibrant and last significantly longer. The Cherrion + Marigold Peach combination creates a dimensional cherry-coral effect that's absolutely striking.

Setting Mist Layering — PIXY + L'Oréal
1 min

Spray PIXY Aqua-Beauty mist first, allow 30 seconds, then follow with L'Oréal Infallible 3-Second Setting Mist. 💡 After setting mist dries, take one final look at your makeup in natural light. If needed, lightly dust any especially shiny spots with a touch more powder. The setting mist will have "baked" the powder into the skin naturally.

Step-by-step evening makeup — from skincare reset to sultry night look.
✦ Evening Routine Philosophy
The evening is your time to experiment with bolder choices — deeper lips, more dramatic eyes, and a more perfected base. The key for combination skin at night is thorough makeup removal, proper double cleansing, and allowing skin to breathe and repair overnight. If wearing makeup to an evening event, prioritise longevity and drama. If staying in, focus on your nighttime skincare ritual.

Refresh Base — TIMEPHORIA Lumina Matte Cushion

For evening, start fresh or refresh your daytime base. If refreshing: blot oily areas, gently touch up with TIMEPHORIA cushion where coverage has faded. 💡 If starting completely fresh from skincare, follow the full base routine from the Morning Glam steps using TIMEPHORIA instead of TIRTIR for that special evening matte-glow look.

Conceal & Perfect — ONLYOU Brightening BB Cream
2 min

Evening lighting can be less forgiving — perfect your under-eye area and any blemishes thoroughly. Use a brightening concealer generously under the eyes and set with loose powder (baking technique) for a luminous, shadow-free under-eye that catches evening light beautifully.

3
Second Date Loose Powder — Evening Setting
2 min

For evenings, powder more thoroughly than in the morning — including baking under eyes and setting the entire face lightly. The translucent powder under evening lighting creates an airbrushed effect and prevents shine that can look oily in photos taken at events.

Sculptural Brows — All Three Brow Products

Layer all three brow products: Hanasui as the filling base, Luxcrime for hair strokes and structure, Azarine for sharp, precise definition at the arch and tail. 💡 For a truly evening-ready brow: extend the tail slightly longer and straighter. Set finished brows with a tiny amount of concealer under the bottom brow line using a flat brush — this sharpens and cleans the line dramatically.

✦ Evening Removal Ritual — Combination Skin
Proper makeup removal and cleansing is arguably more important than application for skin health. For combination skin, a thorough double cleanse removes all makeup, SPF, and sebum, preventing breakouts and ensuring your nighttime skincare products can penetrate effectively.
1
Micellar Water / Cleansing Oil — First Cleanse
2–3 min

Begin with an oil-based cleanser or micellar water to dissolve all makeup, sunscreen, and the setting mists. The "like dissolves like" principle means oil-based removers are superior at lifting foundation, cushion, and waterproof products. Massage gently in circular motions all over the face for 60 full seconds — don't rush this step.

💡 Pay extra attention to brow areas (3 brow products are stubborn), under eyes, and the T-zone where setting mists and powder build up most. A cotton pad soaked in micellar water is ideal for the eye area specifically.
2
Gentle Foaming Cleanser — Second Cleanse
2 min

Follow with a gentle, water-based foaming or gel cleanser. This removes any remaining residue left from the first cleanse, cleanses pores deeply, and balances the skin. For combination skin, use a gentle foaming cleanser that removes excess oil from T-zone without stripping cheeks. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.

💡 Water temperature matters — hot water strips natural oils; cold water doesn't cleanse thoroughly. Lukewarm is ideal. Rinse for a full 30 seconds to ensure no cleanser residue remains.
3
Toner — Balance & Prep
1 min

Apply a balancing toner on a cotton pad or by pressing with hands. Hydrating toners with hyaluronic acid, centella, or niacinamide are ideal for combination skin — they restore pH balance after cleansing and prepare skin to absorb subsequent serums and moisturizers more effectively.

4
Serum & Moisturizer — Overnight Repair
3 min

Apply targeted serums (niacinamide for pore refinement, hyaluronic acid for hydration) while skin is still slightly damp from toner. Follow with a night moisturizer — slightly richer than your daytime formula. Combination skin benefits from a light-medium weight night cream applied all over, with a richer eye cream patted under the eyes.

💡 Skin repairs and regenerates most actively between 10pm–2am. A consistent evening skincare routine maximizes this natural repair window. Never sleep in makeup — even one night can cause breakouts and accelerate skin aging.
5
Lip Overnight Care
30 sec

After all evening products are removed, apply a generous layer of a rich overnight lip mask or balm. The SOMETHINC Ceraplump Lip Balm works beautifully overnight — the ceramides repair the lip barrier while you sleep, especially important after wearing long-wearing lip stains which can be drying over time.

💡 Apply lip care as the very last step — after face moisturizer — to avoid any products transferring onto your pillowcase and causing breakouts around the mouth area.
✦ Midday Touch-Up
Quick refresh tips to keep your look pristine from morning to evening.
✦ Combination Skin Midday Reality
By midday, combination skin typically shows T-zone shine while cheeks remain comfortable. The key is refreshing without disrupting what's already set — less is more. Never pile on more powder over sebum, and always blot before adding any product. For the quickest refresh, the setting mist alone is often enough.
🧻
Step 1 — Blot First

Always start with a blotting paper before anything else. Press gently on the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) to absorb excess sebum. Never rub — rubbing disturbs your makeup. Blotting papers are superior to adding more powder on top of oil, which creates a cakey, uneven result.

  • Gently press blotting paper on T-zone for 3–5 seconds per spot
  • Do not rub or slide the paper
  • Check nose bridge and chin especially
  • Discard paper after use — never reuse it
💧
Step 2 — Setting Mist Refresh

After blotting, a light spritz of PIXY Aqua-Beauty Mist revives the makeup, reducing any powdery or dull appearance while controlling further shine. Hold 20cm away and mist lightly — one or two spritzes is sufficient. This alone often restores the morning look beautifully.

  • Shake PIXY mist before use
  • Close eyes, mist from 20cm in a T or X pattern
  • Let dry naturally — 20–30 seconds
  • Optional: follow with L'Oréal mist for extended longevity
🌸
Step 3 — Powder Touch-Up (If Needed)

If shine persists after blotting and misting, use Second Date Loose Powder very lightly on T-zone only. Use a small fluffy brush and press (not sweep) gently onto shiny areas. The translucent formula blends seamlessly without adding visible colour or appearing cakey over existing makeup.

  • Pick up minimal powder on brush, tap off excess
  • Press and roll gently — never sweep or drag
  • T-zone only: forehead, nose bridge, chin
  • Follow with a light PIXY mist spritz to melt powder naturally
💄
Step 4 — Lip Refresh

Lips typically need the most attention midday, especially after eating and drinking. The approach depends on your lip product. For stain wearers, simply reapply a light layer of your gloss or oil on top. For balm wearers, blot remaining product and reapply fresh colour.

  • Blot away any remaining product with a clean tissue
  • Reapply NIVEA Smoky Rose or Ceraplump as base
  • Layer Glad2Glow Pumpkin Frost or NPure Marigold Peach for shine
  • For bold lips: reapply Time Phoria Cherrion stain and top with oil
✏️
Step 5 — Brow Touch-Up (If Needed)

Brows may fade or shift slightly by midday, especially in the oilier T-zone area where they're anchored. If brows look sparse or faded, a quick touch-up with the Luxcrime Dark Brown pencil or Azarine definer restores definition without requiring full brow reconstruction.

  • Check brow tail first — this fades fastest on oily skin
  • Add light strokes with Luxcrime or Azarine only where needed
  • Brush through with a clean spoolie to blend and shape
  • No need to start over unless brows have completely faded
🌟
Essential Midday Kit

Keep this curated kit in your bag for any-time touch-ups. These are the five products that handle every midday emergency for combination skin.

  • Blotting papers — always the first step
  • PIXY Aqua-Beauty Protecting Mist (travel size if available)
  • Second Date Loose Powder with small brush
  • NIVEA Smoky Rose or Glad2Glow Pumpkin Frost for lips
  • Luxcrime Dark Brown brow pencil for quick brow rescue
