Combination Skin

Understanding your canvas

What is Combination Skin?

Combination skin features an oily T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) alongside normal-to-dry cheeks. It is the most common skin type and requires a balanced approach — controlling shine in oil-prone zones while keeping other areas properly hydrated. Every product in this collection has been thoughtfully chosen to serve both needs simultaneously, without over-drying or overloading the complexion.

✨

T-Zone Oiliness

Forehead, nose, and chin overproduce sebum — causing shine, enlarged pores, and faster makeup breakdown. Matte-finish and oil-controlling products are key for these areas.

💧

Dry Cheeks

Cheeks can feel tight, look dull, or flake — especially in air-conditioning or cool weather. Serum-infused and hydrating formulas help maintain moisture balance here.

🔬

Enlarged Pores

Visible mainly on the nose and chin. Blurring serums, pore-minimizing tints, and finely-milled translucent powder help create a smooth, refined appearance.

⚖️

Balancing Act

Avoid heavy, occlusive formulas on the T-zone and harsh drying agents on the cheeks. Multi-tasking hybrid formulas work best overall for combination skin.

☀️

Sun Protection

Lightweight, non-comedogenic SPF is essential. Combination skin benefits from both physical mineral sunscreens and convenient spray formats that won't block pores.

🌿

Key Routine Tips

Apply heavier coverage selectively, concentrate translucent powder mainly on the T-zone, and use setting mists that lock makeup without amplifying shine-prone areas.

Full Application

Morning routine, step by step

01
Sunscreen First

Apply YOU Sunbrella Physical Sunscreen

Begin with clean, moisturized skin. Apply YOU Sunbrella Physical Sunscreen evenly across the entire face and neck as the absolute last skincare step. Use gentle upward spreading motions or light patting. Wait 1–2 minutes for it to fully set — it creates a subtle priming base that helps makeup adhere better. Apply slightly less to the T-zone if concerned about extra oiliness.

02
Optional Pre-Base

Apply ONLYOU BB Concealer as a Base Layer

Using a fingertip or small brush, dab the ONLYOU Concealer Brightening & Isolating BB Cream onto key areas — blemishes, redness patches, and under the eyes. Blend outward gently. As an isolating formula, it also creates a subtle priming layer across the T-zone that helps all subsequent products adhere evenly and last longer throughout the day.

03
Choose Your Base

Apply Your Foundation or Skin Tint

Full Coverage / Glam Day: Use the TIRTIR Mask Fit Red Cushion (17C Porcelain) for maximum staying power and a luminous finish, or the SKINTIFIC Cover Glow Cushion (Vanilla) for a dewy, glowing look. Press the puff onto the cushion, then apply by dabbing gently from the centre of the face outward and blending at the hairline and jaw.

Natural / Minimal Day: Apply the ESQA Minimalist Blurring Serum Skin Tint (Vanilla) using fingertips or a damp beauty sponge. Blend evenly for a skin-like, pore-blurred finish. The serum actives continue treating your skin throughout wear.

04
Spot Conceal

Touch Up Targeted Areas with ONLYOU

After foundation, revisit any areas that still show discolouration, active spots, or dark circles using the ONLYOU BB Concealer. Use a fingertip with light stippling motions to blend the edges seamlessly. For under-eye application, gently tap — never drag — to prevent creasing. Set these areas immediately with a small amount of powder to lock them in place.

05
Powder Setting

Set the T-Zone with Second Date Loose Powder

Load a fluffy powder brush, tap off the excess, and apply the Second Date Loose Powder (Translucent) with light sweeping then pressing motions over the forehead, nose, and chin. For combination skin, intentionally skip heavy powder on the cheeks — allow them to retain their natural glow or the dewy finish of the cushion. This step is crucial for preventing mid-day T-zone oil breakthrough.

06
Final Lock-In

Set the Look with Mist

Hold L'Oréal Infallible 3-Second Setting Mist 8–10 inches from your face, close your eyes and mouth, and spray in a T or figure-8 motion for full coverage. Let it air-dry completely — do not fan, touch, or pat. This step melds all layers together, minimizes any powder patchiness, and locks the entire look. Optionally follow with 1–2 sprays of PIXY Aqua-Beauty Matte Mist if you want an extra matte-fresh, oil-controlled finish on top.

Mid-Day Refresh

Reapplication & touch-up strategy

🧴

1. Blot First — Always

Before adding any new product, gently press a blotting paper or folded tissue onto the T-zone to absorb excess sebum. Use a pressing motion — never wipe — to remove oil without disturbing the foundation underneath. Skipping this step causes product build-up and patchy reapplication.

☀️

2. Reapply SPF

Sunscreen effectiveness diminishes within 2 hours, especially with sweating or touching. Spray Bening's Sunscreen Spray from about 8–10 inches away in a circular motion across the face. It glides over makeup without disturbing the base. Pat gently after misting to help absorption. Do this even on cloudy days.

💦

3. Refresh with PIXY Mist

Spritz the PIXY Aqua-Beauty Matte Mist 2–3 times in a broad arc across the face to revive moisture on dry cheek areas while simultaneously controlling new oil on the T-zone. The matte finish formula re-balances combination skin beautifully. Allow to dry naturally for 20–30 seconds.

🌫️

4. Light Powder Touch-Up

If the T-zone remains visibly shiny after blotting and misting, press a very small amount of Second Date Loose Powder (Translucent) onto the forehead and nose only. Use a compact brush or the container's sponge. One light pass is enough — over-powdering creates a cakey, unnatural finish that is harder to correct.

🔴

5. Targeted Cushion Touch-Up

For areas where foundation has worn away or faded — typically around the nose crease, chin, or any high-movement areas — lightly tap the TIRTIR Red Cushion or SKINTIFIC Cushion once onto those spots only. Blend edges with a clean fingertip. Avoid a full re-application over the whole face, which causes uneven layering.

💨

6. Re-Seal Everything

Finish the touch-up routine with 1–2 mists of L'Oréal 3-Second Setting Mist to re-seal all layers and restore the cohesive, fresh-from-application look. Hold 8–10 inches away, mist lightly, and allow to air-dry. Your refreshed look should last another 3–4 hours.
