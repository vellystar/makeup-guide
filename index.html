✦ Know Your Skin

Combination Skin Guide

Understanding your skin type is the foundation of every good routine.

✦ You Have Combination Skin

Combination skin is characterized by an oily T-zone (forehead, nose, and chin) alongside normal-to-dry cheeks and outer areas. This is one of the most common skin types, requiring a balanced approach — controlling shine where needed while maintaining hydration elsewhere.

🔬
Characteristics
  • Oily T-zone (forehead, nose, chin)
  • Normal to dry cheeks & temples
  • Enlarged pores around the nose
  • Prone to blackheads in T-zone
  • Makeup tends to slide off T-zone
  • Dry patches may appear on cheeks
  • Skin changes with seasons & humidity
🧪
Key Ingredients to Look For
  • Niacinamide — Balances oil, minimizes pores
  • Hyaluronic Acid — Hydrates without heaviness
  • Ceramides — Strengthens skin barrier
  • Salicylic Acid — Unclogs pores (spot-use)
  • Green Tea Extract — Controls sebum
  • Zinc — Regulates oil production
  • Vitamin C — Brightens & evens tone
🚫
Ingredients to Avoid
  • Heavy oils (coconut, mineral oil) on T-zone
  • Alcohol-heavy toners (strips skin)
  • Thick, occlusive creams on oily areas
  • Harsh scrubs (damages barrier)
  • Silicone-heavy formulas (can clog pores)
  • Overly mattifying products on dry zones
🌤️
Morning Skin Habits
  • Gentle cleanser — don't over-strip
  • Lightweight hydrating toner
  • Niacinamide serum on T-zone
  • Light moisturizer (gel or lotion formula)
  • SPF 30–50 daily — non-negotiable
  • Blot T-zone before makeup
  • Primer on oily zones only
🌙
Evening Skin Habits
  • Double cleanse — oil cleanser first
  • Hydrating toner/essence
  • Treatment serums (Vitamin C, retinol)
  • Eye cream if needed
  • Richer moisturizer on dry zones
  • Lightweight gel on oily zones
  • Never sleep with makeup on
💄
Makeup Tips for Combo Skin
  • Use a mattifying or pore-minimizing primer
  • Apply foundation in thin, buildable layers
  • Powder the T-zone only
  • Avoid over-powdering dry cheeks
  • Setting spray locks everything in
  • Blotting papers > reapplying powder mid-day
  • Cream products can be patchy on oily skin

Undertone Reference Guide

🌅
Warm Undertone
Yellow, peachy, or golden hues beneath skin. Looks best in gold jewelry. Veins appear green. Best shades: beige, caramel, honey, warm ivory, vanilla.
🌊
Cool Undertone
Pink, red, or bluish hues beneath skin. Looks best in silver jewelry. Veins appear blue/purple. Best shades: porcelain, rose beige, pink-toned ivory.
⚖️
Neutral Undertone
Mix of warm and cool. Flattering in both gold & silver. Veins appear blue-green. Most flexible shade range — can wear both warm and cool tones.
💌
Your Profile
Based on your shades (Vanilla, 17C Porcelain), you lean neutral-to-cool, fair to light depth. The 17C indicates a cool preference for base, while Vanilla adds warm flexibility.

✅ Do

  • Multi-mask (clay on T-zone, hydrating mask on cheeks)
  • Use separate products for different zones
  • Blot excess oil throughout the day
  • Stay hydrated — dehydration worsens oiliness
  • Use SPF every single day
  • Cleanse gently — twice a day maximum
  • Choose non-comedogenic makeup formulas

⚠️ Avoid

  • Over-washing — triggers more oil production
  • Skipping moisturizer on oily areas
  • Heavy, creamy foundations on T-zone
  • Applying heavy powder all over face
  • Touching your face throughout the day
  • Using the same product on all zones
  • Skipping SPF to avoid breakouts

✦ Texture & Finish Guide

Types of Makeup Finishes

Understanding finish types helps you pick the right product for every mood, occasion, and skin zone.

Matte
Flat · No Shine

Zero light reflection — absorbs oil and cancels shine completely. Creates a flat, velvety, editorial look. Best for oily and combination T-zones. Can look flat or aging on dry skin if over-applied.

Your products: TIMEPHORIA Matte Cushion · Second Date Powder · PIXY Matte Mist · Pramy Matte Mist

Best for: Oily · Combo (T-zone) · Long days outdoors

Satin
Soft Sheen · Skin-Like

Sits between matte and dewy — a smooth, refined sheen that mimics healthy natural skin. The most universally flattering finish. Doesn't look flat or greasy. Forgives texture and dryness equally well.

Your products: SKINTIFIC Cover Glow Cushion (glow-satin) · ONLYOU BB Cream

Best for: All skin types · Everyday wear · Versatile events

Natural / Skin-Like
Seamless · Second Skin

Lets your real skin show through — looks like you're not wearing makeup at all. Sheer-to-light coverage with a skin-blurring effect. Imperfections are softened, not hidden. The "no-makeup makeup" ideal.

Your products: ESQA Minimalist Serum Skin Tint · TIRTIR Mask Fit (skin-meld effect)

Best for: Good skin days · Normal · Minimal makeup moments

Dewy / Luminous
Glow · Radiant · Fresh

Reflects light softly for a healthy, hydrated glow. Skin looks plump and lit-from-within. Great for dry and normal skin. For combination skin, apply only on cheeks and dry zones — keep T-zone matte to avoid looking oily.

Your products: ESQA Skin Tint · NPure Glow Oil (cheeks) · L'Oreal Setting Mist (luminous lock)

Best for: Dry · Normal · Combo cheeks only

Soft Matte / Lumina Matte
Velvety · Refined Glow

A modern evolved matte — controls shine without looking flat. Has a very subtle inner radiance that prevents the powdery, aged look of traditional matte. The best of both worlds for combination skin.

Your products: TIMEPHORIA Timeless Lumina Matte Cushion (signature finish)

Best for: Combo · Oily · Warm climates (Jakarta humidity)

Shimmer / Frost
Sparkle · Dimensional

Fine shimmer particles catch and reflect light for a glittery, dimensional effect. Used on eyes and lips for drama and depth. "Frost" variants have a cooler, icy shimmer quality. Best used on non-oily areas to prevent glitter migration.

Your products: JADED Palette (shades 01, 03, 05, 09) · Glad2Glow Pumpkin Frost · Time Phoria Stellar Dust

Best for: Eyes & Lips · Evening looks · Targeted highlight application

Metallic / Foil
Mirror-Like · Bold

High-intensity, mirror-like reflection. Unlike shimmer, metallic finishes are opaque and foiled — they completely coat the surface. Used on eyes for maximum drama and editorial impact. Apply with a flat brush or fingertip for the highest intensity.

Your products: JADED Palette shade 05 Copper Glow (duochrome metallic effect)

Best for: Evening · Events · Statement eye looks

Glossy / Glassy
Juicy · Shine · Plumping

A wet, high-shine finish that makes lips look plumper and fuller. Glossy finishes reflect a lot of light, creating a "glass lip" effect. Usually paired with a matte or stained lip underneath for longevity. The most youthful of lip finishes.

Your products: NPure Glow Oil (lips) · Glad2Glow Lip Serum (glassy tint) · Nivea Rich Care & Color

Best for: Daytime · Romantic looks · Layering over stains

Stain / Blotted
Diffused · Long-Lasting

Color that sinks into the skin or lips for a lived-in, effortless look. Applied and blotted for a natural flush effect with exceptional wear. Unlike lipstick, stains don't sit on top — they become part of the skin. Resistant to eating, drinking, and touching.

Your products: Time Phoria Stellar Dust Lip Stain (Cherrion) · SOMETHINC Ceraplump (sheer stain)

Best for: Long wear · Natural makeup · Active days

Duochrome / Multichrome
Colour-Shifting · Unique

Shifts between two or more colors depending on the angle of light. Creates a dimensional, otherworldly effect. Most visible on the eyes. Looks different in sunlight vs. artificial light — always test before an event.

Your products: JADED Palette shade 05 Copper Glow (gold-to-copper duochrome shift)

Best for: Creative looks · Events · Photography

🎨
Finish Layering Strategy for Combination Skin
  • T-Zone: Always matte or soft-matte. Use TIMEPHORIA or finish with Second Date Powder + PIXY/Pramy mist.
  • Cheeks: Satin or dewy — SKINTIFIC glow cushion or NPure Oil pressed only here.
  • Eyes: Matte in the crease, shimmer/metallic on the lid, stain or matte on lower lash.
  • Lips: Stain base (Time Phoria) + gloss top (NPure Oil) = longest-wearing glossy stain result.
  • Overall seal: L'Oreal Setting Mist locks finishes in place without disturbing them.

✦ End of Day

Makeup Removal Tutorial

The most important step of your day — done properly, in the right order, without damaging your skin.

✦ Why Proper Removal Matters for Combination Skin

Leaving makeup on overnight — even just a little — clogs pores, traps sebum in your T-zone, and causes breakouts. For combination skin, improper removal is one of the biggest triggers for both congestion and dryness. The double cleanse method removes every layer without stripping the skin barrier. Never tug, rub, or use harsh wipes as your only method.

1
Remove Eye Makeup First
Micellar Water or Dedicated Eye Makeup Remover
Always start with the eyes. Saturate a soft cotton pad with micellar water or an oil-based eye makeup remover. Hold the pad gently over the closed eye for 10–15 seconds — don't rub immediately. Let the product dissolve the eyeshadow, mascara, and brow product first. Then use a soft downward swipe to remove. For stubborn eyeshadow (especially the JADED darker shades 08 and 09), use a pointed cotton bud soaked in remover to target the outer V and lower lash line precisely.
Never rub the eye area. The skin there is the thinnest on your face and repeated tugging causes premature fine lines and sagging.
2
Remove Lip Color
Oil Cleanser or Micellar Water on Cotton Pad
The Time Phoria Stellar Dust Lip Stain (Cherrion) and the NPure Glow Up Oil are both long-wearing — don't try to wipe them off dry. Apply a small amount of oil cleanser or balm directly to the lips, massage gently in circular motions with your fingertip for 20 seconds to dissolve the stain and any lip balm layers, then wipe away with a soft damp cotton pad. Repeat if the Cherrion stain has deeply set. Your lips should feel soft and clean, not sore.
Lip stains are designed to resist wiping — work with oil, not against the formula. Patience here saves your lip skin from dryness and micro-abrasions.
3
First Cleanse — Oil/Balm Cleanse
Oil Cleanser or Cleansing Balm (Dry Hands, Dry Face)
With dry hands on a dry face, scoop or pump a generous amount of oil cleanser or cleansing balm and massage all over the face in slow, circular motions for a full 60 seconds. This breaks down the cushion foundations (TIRTIR, SKINTIFIC, TIMEPHORIA), concealer (ONLYOU), setting sprays (PIXY + L'Oreal), and the NPure face oil. Pay extra attention to: hairline, around the nose, jaw, and any areas where powder has settled. The T-zone needs longer massage — makeup mixes with sebum and sits deeply in pores there. Emulsify with a few drops of water until it turns milky, then rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water.
Don't skip this step even on "light makeup" days. Sunscreen alone requires oil cleansing to remove properly — water-based cleansers alone cannot break down SPF or silicone-based formulas.
4
Second Cleanse — Water-Based Cleanser
Gentle Foaming or Gel Cleanser
After the oil cleanse, follow with a gentle water-based cleanser to remove any remaining oil residue, leftover product, and deeper surface debris. Lather in palms first, then apply to face with gentle circular motions for another 30–45 seconds. Rinse thoroughly with lukewarm water — never hot (it strips the skin barrier and worsens oil production in the T-zone). Pat dry with a clean, soft towel — never rub. For combination skin, this second cleanse targets any leftover foundation pigment in pores that the oil cleanse may have loosened but not fully rinsed.
After double cleansing, skin should feel clean and comfortable — not squeaky, tight, or stripped. Tightness = barrier damage = more oil production.
5
Brow Pencil Cleanup
Micellar Water on Cotton Bud
After cleansing, check the brow area in good lighting. Brow pencil (especially Luxcrime Dark Brown and Azarine Eyeppuccino) can leave residue along the brow edges and on the skin below/above the brow. Use a cotton bud saturated with micellar water to clean up the brow edges with precision — this also defines the brow shape cleanly. Wipe with a small downward motion below the brow arch and a gentle upward motion above for a neat, clean finish to your cleanse.
6
Check & Spot-Clean
Micellar Water + Cotton Bud for Touch-Ups
Under good lighting, inspect: hairline (powder and foundation often accumulate here), sides of the nose (hardest area to fully cleanse — cushion and skin tint settle into pores here), the under-chin area, and ear perimeter. Use a cotton bud with micellar water for precision cleanup in these spots. A common mistake is thorough face cleansing but missing the hairline — leaving residue here causes hairline breakouts over time.
Check your towel after patting dry — if it shows significant color transfer, your cleanse wasn't complete. Re-cleanse the areas that transferred.
7
Toner — pH Rebalance
Hydrating Balancing Toner
Immediately after cleansing, apply a hydrating toner while skin is still slightly damp to restore skin's pH balance. Cleansing temporarily raises skin's pH — toner brings it back to the ideal slightly acidic level (4.5–5.5) where the skin barrier functions best and where your subsequent skincare products absorb most effectively. For combination skin, pat toner into skin with palms rather than cotton pads — this uses less product and is gentler. Apply an extra press on drier cheek areas.
8
Lip Overnight Treatment
Glad2Glow Brightening Lip Serum or Nivea Rich Care & Color (Smoky Rose)
After your lips are fully clean, apply a generous layer of the Glad2Glow Brightening Lip Serum. Let the niacinamide and Vitamin C complex work overnight to gradually brighten lip pigmentation while you sleep. If skin feels very dry, use the Nivea Rich Care & Color as a thick sleeping lip mask — the rich formula creates an occlusive layer that locks in moisture. Consistent overnight lip care gives visibly smoother, softer, more evenly toned lips within weeks.
This is when brightening ingredients work best — overnight your lips aren't being exposed to food, drinks, or environmental factors that break down active ingredients.
9
Continue with PM Skincare
Essence → Treatment Serum → Eye Cream → Zone-Targeted Moisturizer
With a clean, pH-balanced canvas, proceed with your evening skincare: hydrating essence, treatment serums (niacinamide on T-zone, retinol or peptides all over), eye cream tapped around the orbital bone, and zone-based moisturizer (lightweight gel on T-zone, richer cream on cheeks). For combination skin, this zone-targeted approach is essential — don't apply the same product thickness everywhere. Skin repairs itself most actively between 10pm–2am, so earlier cleansing means better treatment absorption.

✅ Removal Do's

  • Always double cleanse after any SPF or makeup
  • Remove eye makeup before face cleansing
  • Use oil first on dry face — never wet
  • Be patient — let products dissolve before wiping
  • Check hairline, jaw, and nose sides carefully
  • Apply skincare within 60 seconds of cleansing
  • Cleanse every single night without exception

⚠️ Removal Don'ts

  • Don't use makeup wipes as your only removal step
  • Don't rub or tug — especially around eyes
  • Don't use hot water — lukewarm only
  • Don't skip removal because you're tired
  • Don't scrub the T-zone to "deep clean" — damages barrier
  • Don't assume a tinted SPF doesn't need oil cleansing
  • Don't dry with a dirty towel — bacteria transfers back to skin

  • L'Oreal Infallible Setting Mist — Locks makeup; oil cleanse is mandatory to dissolve this film-forming formula.
  • Time Phoria Stellar Dust Lip Stain (Cherrion) — Stains lips by design. Multiple oil-cleanse rounds on the lips may be needed. Don't panic — it will come off.
  • JADED Eyeshadow (Shades 08 & 09) — Dark pigmented shades; use a dedicated eye remover and let it sit before wiping. Cotton bud for precision cleanup in the outer corner.

Smudged liner migrates into fine lines around the eye; a soaked cotton bud cleans these precisely without disturbing surrounding skin.
  • Luxcrime Dark Brown Brow Pencil — Long-wearing; cotton bud with micellar water is the most effective tool for precise brow cleanup without disturbing surrounding skin.

✦ Stay Fresh

Mid-Day Touch-Up Guide

Keep your makeup fresh and your skin comfortable through Jakarta's heat and humidity.

✦ Combination Skin Mid-Day Rule

The golden rule: always BLOT before you touch up. Adding more powder over oil creates a cakey, uneven mess. Blot first, then lightly touch up only where needed. Your PIXY mist can refresh the whole face without disturbing makeup. Less is more for midday — you're refreshing, not reapplying.

1
🧻 Blot the T-Zone First

Use Clean & Clear Oil Control Film — gently press (never rub) onto forehead, nose, and chin. The blue micro-powder film absorbs excess oil and leaves a soft finish. This is always the FIRST step of any midday touch-up. Never skip this or you'll layer powder over oil and create cakey texture.

2
💧 Refresh with Mist

Mist Pramy Moisturizing Setting Spray or PIXY Aqua-Beauty Protecting Mist over your face from 20–25cm distance. Close eyes and spray in T then X pattern. This refreshes the look, controls any remaining shine, and revives makeup without disturbing it. Let air dry naturally. Do this even if you don't do any other touch-up steps.

3
🌸 Light Cushion Touch-Up

If coverage has faded significantly, use the cushion puff (clean side) to very lightly pat TIRTIR or SKINTIFIC only on areas that need it — usually under-eyes and around blemishes. Don't press hard or you'll disturb the existing makeup. Pat gently 2–3 times at maximum. Avoid over-foundation of the T-zone.

4
✨ Powder Only if Needed

If T-zone is still shiny after blotting and misting, apply a tiny amount of Second Date Translucent Powder only on the oiliest spots with a small brush. Use the lightest hand possible — 1–2 gentle sweeps. Avoid the cheeks entirely unless they have dry patches needing setting. Over-powdering mid-day is the most common mistake for combination skin.

5
💋 Refresh Your Lips

Reapply SOMETHINC Ceraplump Tinted Lip Balm (Valentine) for SPF protection and fresh color. If wearing Time Phoria Cherrion stain, reapply a thin layer and blot with a tissue. Top with Glad2Glow Pumpkin Frost for a fresh, luminous finish. The Nivea Smoky Rose is great for a quick, low-maintenance refresh that also moisturizes.

6
✦ Final Seal

Finish midday touch-up with one more light spritz of L'Oreal Infallible 3-Second Setting Mist to seal everything back together and give a freshly-applied finish. Hold at arm's length, mist lightly, and allow to dry. This is the magic step that makes midday touch-ups look seamless rather than layered.

⏰
When to Touch Up (Timing Guide)
  • After 3–4 hours: Blot T-zone + PIXY mist refresh (minimal touch-up)
  • After 5–6 hours: Full midday touch-up routine (blot → mist → light cushion → powder if needed → L'Oreal seal)
  • Before an event/meeting: Blot → mist → lightly press cushion under eyes → lip refresh
  • After eating/drinking: Blot lips gently, reapply lip product, check base around mouth and nose area
  • Outdoor heat exposure: Immediate blotting + PIXY mist — sunscreen reapplication also needed
